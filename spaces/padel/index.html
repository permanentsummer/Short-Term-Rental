    How we deliver

    Supply only, or supply + installation

    Supply only

    Courts are produced to order and delivered ex-works or to your port, with packing lists, certificates and customs codes. Your local contractor installs them.

    Supervised installation

    Two specialists from the manufacturer come to site and install the courts with your local crew, so the job is done right first time.

    FAQ

    Good questions

    Ask us anything else on WhatsApp or by email.

    How long does it take?

    About 5 weeks of production for courts and 2–4 months for covers, plus shipping to your site.

    Can courts go near the sea or in cyclone zones?

    Yes. We recommend Brutux with anti-corrosion paint, and covers whose canvas can be removed before a cyclone.

    What foundations do I need?

    A flat concrete slab with a slight slope for drainage. We send the technical drawings with your quote.

    How is it shipped?

    In containers from Valencia. Unloading needs a crane on site, arranged by the client.
