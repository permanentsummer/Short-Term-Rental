FAQ

Good questions

Ask us anything else on WhatsApp or by email.

Do you supply certificates for each item?

Yes. Every item comes with its EN 1176 certificate, technical sheet and installation manual.

Which surfacing do I need under a playground?

It depends on the fall height of each item. We recommend EPDM rubber or synthetic turf on shock pads; see our safety flooring range.

Is it suitable near the sea?

Yes, with stainless steel, HDPE panels and treated timber. Tell us your site and we will pick the right finishes.
