How much does a padel court really cost?

Court models, covers, foundations, shipping and installation: every cost line explained, with real price ranges.

“How much is a padel court?” is the first question every hotel, club and developer asks us. The honest answer: the court itself is only part of the budget. Here is every cost line, with the prices we actually quote.

1. The court

A complete court includes the steel structure, tempered glass, mesh, 8 LED floodlights, 2 entrances and textured turf with silica. Prices are per court, ex-works Spain:

ModelTypeFrom (excl. VAT)
OlimpiaClassic, 10 mm glass€21,040
PanoramixPanoramic, 12 mm glass€23,570
BrutuxReinforced panoramic€26,820

Panoramic courts cost more but look premium and give spectators a clear view. Near the sea or on windy sites, the reinforced Brutux with anti-corrosion paint is the safer choice.

2. Installation

You can use your own contractor (supply only) or ask for supervised installation: two specialists from the manufacturer install the court with your local crew. We quote supervision at €2,875 per court, travel and accommodation excluded.

3. A cover (optional)

A cover turns a court into a year-round business. An aluminium One-Pro cover starts at €59,800, the larger One-Pro XL at €64,690, plus assembly of about €5,750 per cover. A shading flap adds €6,900. Allow 2–4 months of production.

4. What is not in the price

  • Foundations: a flat concrete slab with a slight slope. Cost depends on your site; get a local quote.
  • Transport: courts ship in containers from Valencia. We quote freight to your exact port or site.
  • Unloading: a crane on delivery day, arranged by the client.
  • Import duties and VAT: depend on your country.

5. A realistic example

Two panoramic courts with supervised installation, no cover: about €52,900 – €55,500 excl. VAT, ex-works, before slab, transport and duties.

Prices are indicative, excl. VAT and may change with steel and glass prices. Use the padel estimator for your own configuration, then ask us for a firm quote including transport.
